Client OLX Brasil
Year 2023 – 2024
Role Lead Product Designer
Scope Research · Experimentation · Growth · Delivery
Context

Influence at every step

With the key objective of increasing buyers and sellers on the platform, we analyzed the main pain points throughout the journey and applied influence techniques such as mental triggers and cognitive biases across the entire buying and selling funnel. Each team was responsible for applying these techniques within their scope and monitoring the impact. Some initiatives were not tested, but others allowed us to quickly leverage important metrics.

01

Listing / Results page

Problem / Opportunity

Buyers consistently find the same ads for their search, affecting engagement with the platform.

🧠 Trigger / Bias

Self-initiated triggers

Users are more likely to interact with prompts that they configure themselves.

Hypothesis

With a "save search" feature linked to notifications and alerts for new ads, we will see 19% of Listing viewers coming from saved searches and an increase in Adviewers making contact in less than 7 days.

02

Product page

Problem / Opportunity

Many buyers avoid messaging via chat and negotiating price because of the advertiser's slow response time in the chat.

🧠 Trigger / Bias

Law of Least Effort

Choose the path of least resistance.

Hypothesis

A quick shortcut to make an offer on the product page will make the negotiation more objective for both buyer and seller, generating more contacts and transactions on the platform.

03

Photo Gallery

Problem / Opportunity

By analyzing heatmaps and session recordings, we observed that most buyers, upon landing on the listing page, immediately click on the image gallery. After viewing all the photos, many of them leave the listing page without taking further action.

🧠 Trigger / Bias

Peak-End Rule

Users are more likely to take action based on how an experience ends, especially after a highly engaging moment.

Hypothesis

Adding a shortcut button and displaying the listing price directly within the photo gallery leverages the Peak-End Rule by turning the final, most engaging moment of the experience into a clear conversion opportunity, increasing purchases and contacts on the platform.
